# UniTask

## About the project

UniTask is a platform created by students for students. It helps students find support from one another and exchange useful skills, time, and experience.

One student can post an announcement describing a task they need help with. Other students can browse the available announcements and offer to complete a task that matches their skills or interests.

## What can students use it for?

UniTask can be used for many everyday, study-related, and practical tasks, such as:

- repairing or setting up a computer or phone;
- helping with software or technology;
- moving things or assembling furniture;
- explaining a difficult study topic;
- helping with a presentation or project;
- completing a small one-time task;
- offering personal skills or advice to other students.

## How it works

1. A student creates an announcement describing the task they need help with.
2. Other students browse the available announcements.
3. An interested student chooses a task and offers their help.
4. The author selects a suitable helper.
5. The students agree on the details and complete the task.
6. After completion, they can leave feedback about their experience.

Every user can both ask for help and help others. This makes UniTask a student community where people can solve problems together, gain experience, and build connections.

## Main goals

- Make it easier for students to find help nearby.
- Give students an opportunity to share their skills.
- Connect people who need assistance with people who can provide it.
- Support cooperation and trust within the student community.

## Project vision

UniTask aims to create a friendly and useful space where students can help each other with everyday and study-related tasks.
